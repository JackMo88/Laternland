# Lanternland

A cosy co-op adventure for two in the browser (PC and phone).
Two little Funkis hatch from the same lantern and relight the Star Lanterns that the Hush snuffed out.

**Play:** https://jackmo88.github.io/Laternland/

## Chapter 1 · Mossnest (version 0.5)

- A full first chapter: Lantern Nest, Mossy Ledge, Mossbottom village, Firefly Grove, Windspire, Grumblewood, Puddle Pond and the Wobble Arena
- Story dialogue with numbered choices, d20 skill checks (Brave, Clever, Charming) and votes where both players decide
- Three art-style shifts: the Glowroot Caves play as a 2D side-scroller, Hush Hollow is drawn in ink, and Pemberton's memory is pixel art
- 8 Glimmling eggs and 7 species, each with its own ability (light, glide, magnet, steady feet, bramble breaking, bouncing)
- Side quests and minigames: parcels, a bake-off, a fishing duel, hide and seek, a missing brooch, fireflies, Wobble Sumo, crystal smashing
- Solo (swap between both Funkis), two players on one device, or online on two devices
- Turnable camera, adjustable interface size, movable and resizable touch buttons

Nobody ever dies in Lanternland: Funkis float back in a bubble, and Glimmlings that tumble too often just go home for a nap.
